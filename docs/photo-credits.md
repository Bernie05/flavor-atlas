# Photo credits

Every recipe photo is bundled in `public/photos/<recipe-slug>.webp` and comes from a freely licensed source (Wikimedia Commons; CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA). The recipe itself stores the short credit (`imageCredit`) and the source page (`imageSourceUrl`), and the app shows both over each photo.

**Changes made to every photo:** resized and cropped to 4:3, converted to WebP. CC BY-SA photos stay under their original license.

Photos were chosen to show the exact dish or regional version. Where Commons had no usable photo of that version, the closest match was used; the Note column says so.

## Adding a photo

1. Find a CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA photo (Wikimedia Commons first) and note the author and license from the file page.
2. Crop to 4:3, resize to 1200px wide and save as WebP (quality about 78) in `public/photos/<recipe-slug>.webp`.
3. Set `imageUrl`, `imageCredit` ("Photo: Author, License") and `imageSourceUrl` on the recipe in `db.seed.json`, and add a row here. `seed.test.ts` fails if the credit, source or file is missing.
